Science is best when it is communicated to the general public, as it may
inspire both the speaker and their audience. That is the idea behind
"Įkvėpti mokslo" ("Inspired by Science"), the open lecture series run by the
[Energy and Technology Museum](https://etm.lt/) (ETM) in Vilnius together
with the [Vilnius City Council](https://vilnius.lt/) and [Vilnius
University](https://www.vu.lt/). Science journalist and writer Goda
Raibytė-Aleksa will moderate, helping the audience ask their questions.

The lectures start today (see, [ETM
website](https://etm.lt/etm-sugrizta-paskaitu-ciklas-ikvepti-mokslo/) and
will take place on every Wednesday up to the 9th of December. Notably, among
diverse group of researchers, there will be two members of our group:

* **On the 11th of November** [Vygintas Gontis](https://gontis.eu/) will tell
  about physics-inspired understanding of Lithuanian economic success.
* **On the 18th of November** [Aleksejus
  Kononovicius](https://kononovicius.lt/) will give a more general
  perspective on how physicists see social phenomena.

Both talks are free and open to everyone, and no specific background in
physical sciences or social sciences is needed. Bring your curiosity and
questions! See you!
