## Society through a physicist eyes

Yesterday, I gave lecture "Society through a physicist eyes: From traffic
jams to market fluctuations" (lt. "Visuomenė fiziko akimis: nuo transporto
kamščių iki rinkų svyravimų") at [Energy and Technology
museum](https://etm.lt). I was pleased to see so much people showing their
curiosity (by attending and asking interesting questions) in this topic. As
usual, I would like to share my
[slides]({static}/uploads/2026/Kononovicius2026ETM.pdf) (though they are in
Lithuanian).

![first slide]({static}/uploads/2026/inspired-by-science-lecture-slides.jpg)

Notably, this time I have made (with considerable help from Anthropic's
[LLM](/tag/large-language-models/) Claude Sonnet 5.5) some interactive
simulations specifically for this event. These are not yet properly prepared
to become their own posts on Physics of Risk (though they will likely be in
the near future), but they are already accessible. You have the app index on
a [dedicated page](https://rf.mokslasplius.lt/uploads/special/etm-2026/).
