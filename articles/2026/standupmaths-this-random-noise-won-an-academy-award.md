look natural. [Perlin noise](/tag/perlin-noise/) is noise which serves as a
comprise between randomness and looking natural. Watch this video by
[standupmaths](https://www.youtube.com/@standupmaths) which explains what
[Perlin noise](/tag/perlin-noise/) is, and why it was awarded an Oscar.
