In this [Numberphile](https://www.youtube.com/@numberphile) video prof.
Holly Krieger discusses an interesting conjecture related to hyperbolic
maths and the Mandelbrot set. It seems that the complex numbers within
different parts (the cardiod, or the disks) of the interior of the
Mandelbrot set, when iterated using the classic rule,
\\\( z\_{i+1} = z\_i^2 + c \\\), approach to an oscillation between finite
number of points. For more details and intuitions watch the video below.
