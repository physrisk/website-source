generated by a non-Poisson process?

## A non-Poisson process

There are multiple ways to construct a non-Poisson process. Though likely,
the simplest way, at least for our purposes, would be to assume that
inter-event time distribution does not follow exponential distribution. So,
let us use Pareto distribution instead,

\begin{equation}
p(x) = \begin{cases}
    \frac{\alpha x\_{\text{min}}^{\alpha}}{x^{\alpha+1}} & \text{if }x\geq x\_{\text{min}},\\\\
    0 & \text{otherwise}.
    \end{cases}
\end{equation}
